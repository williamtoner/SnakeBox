William Toner

I'm a machine learning researcher at Sakana AI in Tokyo. Before that I spent two years at Huawei working on efficient LLM inference, after a PhD at the University of Edinburgh. I've written about time series forecasting, faster language models and learning from noisy labels. In my spare time I like computational puzzles.

    A new term of OEIS A029495

    Numbers k that divide the reversed binary concatenation of 1 to k. The first three terms, 1, 5 and 337, had stood alone; a parallel GPU search found the fourth, 58088321, and showed there are no others below 108.
